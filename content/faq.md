Start with what you know. These are the questions people ask about a sliding door, answered with the parts of the work that hold true for any opening rather than a guess about yours.

The order follows a project. Whether a barn-style door suits the room at all, what to have ready before you get in touch, then the clearances and measurements that decide what fits, then the hardware and how the door moves, then what it is like to live with once it is hung.

Anything tied to Jeff’s business, such as what he supplies, where he works, and what a project costs, belongs in the conversation with him. Set aside some time for it. He will have thoughts.
