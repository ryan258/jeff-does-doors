## A door can change the whole room

Jeff Does Doors specializes in barn-style doors. That focus gives this site its starting point: the opening, the style, the visible hardware, and how you want the room to feel.

## Meet Jeff

Jeff’s own introduction belongs here. His background, how he got into the work, and what customers can expect still need to come from him. This draft does not invent a business history, qualifications, or years of experience.

## Bring your idea

![Illustrative workshop view of timber slabs, carpenter's plane, and black sliding hardware on a workbench](/images/barn-door-workshop.webp)

A photo of the opening and a style you like can start the conversation. Add your town and what you hope to change. Specific installation, supply, custom-building, and repair arrangements need confirmation before the service list is finalized.
