## Visible hardware deserves a little thought

A barn door’s track and pull are part of the finished appearance. Think about the other finishes in the room and whether you want the hardware to blend in or stand out. Jeff has an opinion about every pull. He will share it, and then ask for yours.

## Choose the system together

Door and hardware choices need to work together. Keep the manufacturer and product details available so the complete setup can be reviewed before buying. Jeff will read the spec sheet, then the fine print, then the fine print’s fine print.

## Explain what you want to improve

![Illustrative close-up of matte black steel spoke roller wheel, steel track rail, and mounting bolts](/images/barn-door-hardware.webp)

If this is an update to an existing door, include what you like about the current setup and what you would change: appearance, feel, or the way it moves. Jeff will want to see the old rollers. Please do not throw them out before he does.
