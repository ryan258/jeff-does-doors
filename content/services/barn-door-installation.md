## Make the doorway part of the room

A barn-style door brings its material, pattern, and visible hardware into the space. Start with the feel you want: simple and clean, warm and rustic, or a finish that matches the room around it.

## Start with the space

Show the full opening and the wall beside it, including trim, switches, and nearby furniture. That context helps explain how you use the room and where you imagine the door sitting when open.

## Talk through the complete look

![Illustrative concept of a modern white sliding barn door on a pantry opening with matte black track](/images/barn-door-pantry.webp)

The door, track, pull, and finish are one visual decision. Keep inspiration photos or product links ready, and confirm who will supply each part before ordering.
