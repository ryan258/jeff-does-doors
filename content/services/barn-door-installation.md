## Give the door room to work

Start with how you use the room, the privacy you need, and the wall where the open door would sit. Include trim, switches, and nearby furniture in a photo of the whole opening.

## Choose the whole look

![Illustrative concept of a modern white sliding barn door on a pantry opening with matte black track](/images/barn-door-pantry.webp)

The door, track, pull, and finish will share the room. Compare them together and confirm who supplies each part before ordering.
