Four project options are offered here, and they differ mainly in where the work starts. A sliding door for an opening that has never had one. A change to a door that is already there, including a swinging door you want to slide instead. The visible hardware on its own. Or an existing door that no longer moves the way it should.

Most projects arrive as one of the four and get named properly in conversation. A replacement can turn out to be a hardware question. An adjustment can turn out to be a clearance problem in the wall above the trim. Jeff will usually know which before he has finished walking up the driveway.

What sits inside each option, including supply, fitting, and finishing, is confirmed with Jeff. Start with the opening and what you want to change, and the right heading usually becomes clear.
