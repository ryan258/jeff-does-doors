## Start with what you notice

A scrape at one point, a new noise, or a change in movement is useful information. You do not need to know the cause before describing the problem.

## Give the existing setup some context

Include the door and hardware details if you have them, when the issue began, and whether anything nearby has changed. A view of the whole opening helps put the close-up details in context.
