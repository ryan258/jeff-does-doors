## Website requests

The hosting provider receives the technical requests needed to serve pages, such as an IP address and browser information. If web fonts are enabled for this site, they load from Google Fonts. Those services have their own data-handling policies.

This site has no configured advertising pixels or analytics tracker. Nothing here follows you around. Jeff will only follow up if you ask him to. If you dismiss the announcement bar, your browser remembers that choice on this device. That note stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere.

## Project briefs

By default, form entries are held in the current page and leaving or reloading can lose them. When device saving is available, you can explicitly opt in to browser storage for this business and site path. Saved drafts include the fields you enter and a save time. They can be restored or deleted from the form. A draft expires after seven days and is removed the next time this site checks it; there is no background deletion while the browser is closed. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to restore it. Browser settings may block or clear storage. The displayed status reports whether a save succeeded. Copying places the brief on your clipboard; downloading creates a text file on your device. Neither action contacts {{< business-name >}}.

Email and text actions open your chosen messaging application with the brief. You decide whether to send it. That application and the recipient then handle the message under their own practices.
