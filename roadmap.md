## Next: prove the working revision

## Next: supply the business facts

Confirm supply/fabrication/installation scope first. Then confirm contacts, coverage, response expectations, exclusions, owner story, authentic photography and usage rights, and any pricing/warranty/payment terms. Keep unconfirmed modules disabled. Record actual reviewed revisions in the evidence register; hashes are not signatures.

## Next: operations and authorized release

Choose the receiving workflow and production host, configure the selected environment and prove receipt/recovery/retention. Review sources, commit only after Ryan delegates it, run the production gate, and publish only after separate authorization. Existing GitHub Pages staging and proposed Cloudflare production are different hosting arrangements.

## After a field trial

Prioritize several openings per job, structured materials/estimating, dimensioned drawings, verified hardware catalog, local photo evidence and a customer handover package according to actual friction. Add an authenticated operator inbox only after the access model is chosen. Keep maintenance refactors incremental and backed by browser evidence.
