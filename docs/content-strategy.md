# Contractor content, discovery and conversion strategy

## One connected customer journey

Search or Maps → relevant service page → credible local proof and answers → contact or prepared message → human follow-up. Each page should help a visitor decide whether this contractor handles the work, serves the property, can substantiate its claims and has a practical next step.

The full master is a capability library. The published client site should reflect the contractor's actual business. Start with the highest-value service and add pages when they have distinct visitor intent and enough real information to justify them.

## Information architecture

| Page | Visitor decision | Required content | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Is this the right type of contractor? | Specialty, confirmed area, concise promise, genuine proof, key services | Priority service or contact |
| Service | Can you handle my project? | Scope, exclusions, site constraints, process, cost factors, questions, relevant work | Service-aware message or call |
| Project | Have you done relevant work? | Actual location at permitted granularity, problem, scope, constraints, photos and documented outcome | Related service |
| Service area | Will you come to this property? | Confirmed towns/state/counties, boundary or travel explanation | Ask about the location |
| About | Who is accountable? | Real people, business background, actual credentials and working approach | Contact |
| Guide | What do I need to understand first? | Direct answer, useful detail, named author/reviewer, actual dates and reliable sources | Relevant service or guide |
| Contact / message | How do we begin? | Working contact, information to prepare, hours and actual follow-up expectations | Explicit handoff |

Use locality-specific landing pages only when the contractor has distinct service information, proof and a real reason for that page. Repeating the same copy with a new town name is not a useful content strategy.

## Persuasive copy pattern

Lead with the work and the customer's desired outcome. Explain the important uncertainty, show evidence, describe what happens next, then ask for the smallest useful action. Keep urgent visitors' phone path visible when a real number exists. Use optional form detail to help qualification without making a long questionnaire the only contact route.

Example draft for the barn-door specialization: “Plan your door with the opening in view. Tell us where it goes and whether you are adding a door, replacing one, or fixing how an existing one slides. We can use that to identify the next questions.” The owner must review any promise about what the contractor will do. Avoid unsupported response times, guaranteed prices, years of experience or outcomes.

Service pages now expose service-specific questions, cost factors and a contextual message. Replace generic sample details with the contractor's actual exclusions, process and proof. The form preserves the selected service and starts unspecified for general inquiries.

## Organic search and AI discovery

Use one clear page per substantial intent, descriptive titles, unique summaries, logical headings, internal links and crawlable text. The service catalog supplies names and search terms consistently to UI, inquiry choices, local search and structured data. Publish real source material: job photos, field observations, named expertise, explicit scope and answers with context.

Google's [AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) ties eligibility to established Search requirements and useful content. Do not promise inclusion in AI answers or rankings from schema, an `llms.txt` file or special wording. The implementation adds no speculative AI-only markup. Google FAQ rich results are no longer a reason to add FAQ schema; keep useful questions visible for people. See [Search documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates).

Article metadata requires actual author, reviewer, publication and modification dates before production. Updating a date without substantively reviewing content is not freshness. Technical/regulatory guidance should identify jurisdiction and direct readers to the current authority. Review structured data against visible facts; never add ratings, addresses or qualifications solely to gain a search appearance.

The master emits canonical URLs, responsive images, fingerprinted assets, business/service identity and breadcrumbs. The output audit checks subpath links and sitemap coverage. The current changes await local execution and device checks; these mechanisms do not establish performance or ranking results.

## Google Business Profile / Maps

Confirm ownership of the existing profile and actual business identity before changing anything. Align the name, contact methods, category, service coverage, website and operating hours with reality. Confirm whether the address is public; a service-area business should not expose a private address through the website simply because an address field exists. Use genuine project photos and accurate services. Request honest reviews through an owner-approved process and respond appropriately.

See [the listing workflow](optimising-the-google-place-listing.md) and [Google's profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177). This implementation changes no Google account or listing. Eligibility, verification and live category selection remain owner/account tasks.

## Content production sequence

1. Confirm contacts, priority service, actual geography and inquiry handling.
2. Replace hero and core service copy; collect real work imagery and permissions.
3. Write one detailed project story per priority service where evidence exists.
4. Publish decision-support guides: cost drivers, preparation, timing, questions, repair versus replacement where professionally reviewed.
5. Add pricing, financing, warranties, emergency work, hiring or promotions only when real terms and maintenance ownership exist.
6. Revisit pages using actual inquiry questions and, after setup, Search Console/Business Profile performance and business outcomes.

Suggested editorial record: target customer question, service ID, intent, owner, source evidence, draft status, technical reviewer, publication date, last substantive review and next-review trigger. Do not turn this into a quota of articles or invented case studies.

## Measurement plan awaiting implementation choices

Begin with visits to priority service pages, contact actions, prepared messages, qualified inquiries and booked work. A click on email is an intent signal; it is not delivery or a sale. Connect tools only after selecting the provider, privacy treatment and receiving workflow. Keep personal project text out of analytics. Establish a baseline, change one meaningful element at a time and assess business results rather than promising a conversion uplift without evidence.
