Preparation guide

Photos to have ready

Before our first conversation or when sharing your room brief, these three quick photos help Jeff check clearances right away. More photos are welcome. Jeff will look at all of them:

  1. 1. Straight-on opening Stand 8 feet back so we see the full opening from floor to ceiling, including existing trim.
  2. 2. The sliding wall run Show the wall space to the left or right where the door will park when open, noting any light switches or outlets.
  3. 3. Header & ceiling gap A photo of the wall area directly above the top casing showing available height to the ceiling.
