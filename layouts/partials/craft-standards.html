Structure

Door construction

Discuss material, construction, weight, and finish together. Check suitability for the room and hardware before settling on the grain.

Mounting

Structural mounting

The rail needs support appropriate to the door and system. Assess the wall and follow the exact mounting instructions; appearance alone cannot establish what is behind it.

Hardware

Compatible moving parts

Select rollers, stops, guides, and any soft-close parts as a compatible system. Keep the model details and instructions with the project.

Alignment

Guidance and alignment

Confirm the required guide, clearances, and adjustment procedure for the selected system. Lenny cannot be talked into a better reading.
