Clearances & fit

What to check before choosing a door.

A sliding barn door needs space to travel and a solid surface to hang from. Here are the four checks to make on your opening.

01

Door overlap

Opening width + 2" to 4" · Opening height + 1"

A barn door hangs on the face of the wall rather than inside the frame. Measure the bare opening, not the casing: adding 2" to 4" to that width covers it by 1" to 2" on each side. Wide or proud casing changes the figure, so note your trim width and let Jeff confirm the final slab size. He will say it could be bigger. It is a Texas thing.

02

Header clearance

Minimum 6" above door trim

The steel rail and roller wheels mount above the door casing. You need at least 6 inches of clear wall space between the top of the opening and the ceiling or crown molding.

03

Slide run space

Full door width along adjacent wall

The wall where the door slides open must be completely flat and unobstructed. Check for light switches, thermostats, heat registers, or artwork that might sit in the path of the slide.

04

Floor & bottom guide

3/8" to 1/2" floor gap · Level floor

A floor stay roller keeps the door plumb and prevents swinging. Modern doors have a hidden groove mortised into the bottom edge that glides quietly over a concealed guide pin.

Not sure if your opening has the clearance?

Snap a photo showing the doorway and the entire wall next to it. Include it when you message Jeff and he can check the layout. He will have already looked at it twice.
