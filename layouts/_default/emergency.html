Get the right help

If anyone is trapped, injured, or unable to leave a room, call 911 first. A website cannot tell whether a door is safe to use. A door off its track can fall. Keep people and pets clear of it until someone has looked at it.

What to have ready when you call

  • The property address and which door it is
  • What you are seeing and hearing, and when it started
  • Whether the door is blocking an exit
  • Photos, only if they can be taken safely

Leave the hardware alone

Do not drill, dismantle, or force a suspect installation. Keep clear and arrange qualified help; no responder or arrival time is confirmed through this site.

Not an emergency?

Use the project brief to organize planned work. It does not book a visit or request urgent assistance.
