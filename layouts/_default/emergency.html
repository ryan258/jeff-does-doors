What to have ready when you call

  • The property address and how to reach the work area
  • What you are seeing and when it started
  • Whether the property currently has usable water and working drains
  • Photos, if it is safe to take them

Before you dig — anywhere

Contact your state’s 811 center before digging. Follow its waiting period and confirm utility responses before work starts. See 811 before-you-dig guidance for the next step in your state.
