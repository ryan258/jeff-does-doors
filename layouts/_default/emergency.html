Get the right help

If anyone is trapped, injured, or unable to leave a room, call 911 first. A website cannot tell whether a door is safe to use. A door off its track can fall. Keep people and pets clear of it until someone has looked at it.

What to have ready when you call

  • The property address and which door it is
  • What you are seeing and hearing, and when it started
  • Whether the door is blocking an exit
  • Photos, if it is safe to take them. Jeff will ask for more.

Before you drill

Walls hold wiring and pipes. If you are tempted to fix a rail yourself, check what is behind the drywall first, or wait for Jeff. He will come. He is probably already nearby.

Not an emergency?

Planned work gets scheduled through the normal estimate path. Jeff will still want to hear about it.
