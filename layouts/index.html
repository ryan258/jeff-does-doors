The big picture

Some doors just open.
Others change the room.

There’s a reason a barn-style door catches your eye. The texture of the wood. The line of the track. The way an ordinary opening becomes part of the room.

That’s the focus at Jeff Does Doors. Start with a room you want to change and a style you like. Then talk through the opening, the hardware, and the details that make it feel at home.
